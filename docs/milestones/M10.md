# M10: Figures

Status: **complete, approved by the owner on 2026-10-03**, tagged `v0.10-m10-figures` (on commit `8fa4ae5`).

## What was built

- **Figures of every finished run** (`qmaws-engine::figures`), drawn after each analysis run in the terminal, the menu and the GUI, and again with `qmaws figures --output <run>`:
  - `halo_tree.svg/.pdf/.png`: the Quartet Halo Tree (plan 4.8.1), with branch colour and width by S1 (or S2 with `--s2`), the halo ring with a dot on values below 0.6, group bands with their names, a title block (run, taxa, quartets, settings, date) and legends;
  - `rectangular_tree.svg/.pdf/.png` (4.8.3): S1/S2 at every node, a halo square and the group beside each name;
  - `tanglegram.svg/.pdf/.png` (4.8.2): our tree against the reference tree, with child orders rotated to reduce crossings, dashed vermillion lines for taxa whose closest relatives differ, and nRF, nQD and MSD in the header (also `report/reference_comparison.tsv`);
  - `interactive_tree.html` (4.8.4): one self-contained file; zoom, pan, taxon search, tooltips, circular or rectangular layout, branch colours by S1, S2 or none;
  - `halo_tree_growth.gif` (4.7): the live provisional trees, then the final tree;
  - `convergence.svg/.pdf/.png` (4.7): nRF of each provisional tree to the final tree;
  - `groups.tsv`: the groups of the bands and their source.
  PNG files have 300 dots per inch for the SVG size at 96 dots per inch. Figures are outside every stage, so the root fingerprint does not change.
- **Group bands from three sources:** a group file (`groups.tsv` in the input folder, or `--groups`); the Open Tree of Life taxonomy on request (`--otl`; `qmaws-data::otl`, API checked against its documentation and live calls; the most specific of genus, family, order, class and phylum with 2 to 7 groups; queries recorded in `audit/otl_taxonomy.json`); automatic clades of the tree.
- **Metrics** (`qmaws-core::metrics`): nQD by enumeration of every quartet (four-point condition; quartets unresolved in the reference counted apart) and the matching split distance with the Hungarian algorithm (raw, OI-15).
- **Colour scales and their check** (`qmaws-viz::colour`): viridis for support, ColorBrewer PuOr for halo values, Okabe–Ito for groups; protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia simulated (Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes 2009, severity 1.0), differences measured as CIEDE2000.
- **Commands:** `qmaws figures` (`--reference`, `--groups`, `--otl`, `--s2`); hidden `qmaws figure-check` (figures for the taxon names of a dataset on a random tree, for readability checks). The GUI offers "Open the Halo Tree" and "Open the interactive tree" after a run.
- `gif` 0.14.2 added (380 crates for the release platforms). Documentation: README "Figures", `docs/USER_GUIDE.md` "Figures", `docs/DESIGN.md` "Figures (M10)", `docs/EXTERNAL_TOOLS.md` (Open Tree of Life), `docs/OPEN_ISSUES.md` OI-15 to OI-17.

## Results

Colour check (`qmaws-viz::colour::check`):

| Vision | Smallest difference between group colours | Support scale: ends differ by | Halo scale: ends differ by |
|---|---|---|---|
| normal | 21.7 | 98.5 | 50.3 |
| protanopia | 12.2 | 86.0 | 54.7 |
| deuteranopia | 11.6 | 79.4 | 57.8 |
| tritanopia | 10.9 | 76.1 | 30.7 |

In every vision the support scale's lightness falls monotonically and the halo scale's rises to the middle and falls after it. The threshold of 10 is this project's choice; tritanopia comes closest (10.9, between two of the seven group colours).

Fish mtDNA against the AFproject reference tree (`report/reference_comparison.tsv`): nRF 0.455 (20 of 44 splits differ, as in M8), nQD 0.371 (4,690 of 12,650 quartets differ), MSD 51 (raw, 25 taxa). The tanglegram marks 10 of 25 taxa; the untangling left 22 crossings (51 with child reversals only, before all rotations were tried). Open Tree of Life (taxonomy 3.7): 23 of 25 species names matched once, 1 had no match (Oreochromis sp-KM2006), 1 more than one; family gives 5 groups.

E. coli–Shigella against the AFproject reference tree: nRF 0.654 (34 of 52 splits differ), nQD 0.416 (9,892 of 23,751 quartets differ), MSD 68 (raw, 29 taxa); the untangling left 31 crossings. S1 ranges from 0.603 to 0.987 and no taxon has a halo value below 0.6; S2 ranges from 0.000 to 1.000 (`run.log`). How these compare with ML-MAWS and the other methods is the subject of M11.

## Known limitations

- MSD is reported raw, as defined in the paper (OI-15); the tanglegram marks taxa whose closest relatives differ (OI-16); the Open Tree of Life is asked only on request (OI-17). All three are the owner's choices.
- The 116-taxon check uses a random tree; the Rhinovirus run itself comes with M11.
- Reference trees from the Open Tree of Life are not downloaded yet; the tanglegram uses the AFproject trees or a tree given with `--reference`.
- Group colours repeat after seven groups (the names stay in the bands and the legend).
- The growth animation needs the frames in `work/`, which is not committed; a run folder without `work/` keeps its committed GIF but cannot draw it again.
- The interactive page was checked in one browser engine.
