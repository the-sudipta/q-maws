# M5: Quartet weighting

Status: **complete, approved by the owner on 2026-10-02**, tagged `v0.5-m05-weighting` (on commit `d49f46c`). The owner accepted the IQ-TREE criterion as met by comparisons A, B and C, with IQ-TREE's lower maxima from its own start reported as a finding.

## What was built

- **Weighting** (`qmaws-core::weight`):
  - W1 votes.
  - The two-state model, with W2-sym (π0 = π1 = 0.5) and W2-emp (π1 = the fraction of 1s in `M_full`), and its 16 pattern probabilities.
  - The conditioned log-likelihood ("not 0000"; for the IQ-TREE cross-check only, also "not 0000 and not 1111").
  - Branch-length fits from three starts.
  - W2a, W2b and W2c.
- **Optimiser** (justified in `docs/DESIGN.md`): projected Newton steps with Levenberg–Marquardt damping on the exact gradient and Hessian in s = exp(−βt), with coordinate sweeps as a fallback, in the bounds [0.000001, 10].
- **W2c**: 100 multinomial resamples by exact conditional binomial draws from SplitMix64, with the per-quartet seed SHA-256(seed ‖ rank) truncated to 64 bits; ties within 10⁻⁸ are split equally.
- **Determinism**: every transcendental function comes from `libm`; sums run in a fixed order; each quartet is computed sequentially.
- **Analysis stage `quartet_weight`**: chunked in the seeded order and resumable. Its stage hash is taken over the weights rounded to 9 significant digits. New `qmaws run` options: `--weighting w2-sym|w2-emp|none` and `--replicates B`.
- **IQ-TREE cross-check**: the hidden commands `iqtree-export` and `iqtree-compare`, `scripts/iqtree_check.sh` and the development workflow `iqtree.yml` (IQ-TREE 3.1.4 on a GitHub runner, as the owner chose).

## Findings

- **IQ-TREE cross-check:** the two likelihood functions agree within IQ-TREE's print precision in all 30 rows. Started from its own default lengths, however, IQ-TREE stops below the optimum in 14 of 30 rows, by up to 0.270115. IQ-TREE itself scores Q-MAWS's lengths higher (`-blfix`), and started from them it stays at Q-MAWS's maximum. Of the 14 rows, 12 were visible in the annotations, and 11 of those 12 are fits of a topology that is not the best for its quartet.
  - IQ-TREE's documentation lists no further option to tighten its branch-length optimisation beyond `-blmin`, `-blmax`, `-blfix` and `-me`, which were used.
  - The plan's wording ("log-likelihoods match within 0.0001") is therefore met for the likelihood function (A, B) and for the optimum when IQ-TREE is started from Q-MAWS's lengths (C). It is not met for IQ-TREE's maximum from its own start. The owner accepted the criterion as met on this basis on 2026-10-02.
- An earlier count of 12 rows, sent to the owner on 2026-10-02, was wrong. GitHub shows at most 10 notices per step, so the rows of quartet 4 under W2-sym were not shown; the correct count, 14, comes from the script's summary line. The script now writes one notice per quartet.
- **Coordinate ascent alone was not enough.** On ridges, where a leaf branch and the internal branch trade off, it reached its cap of 5,000 sweeps and stopped up to 0.0000003 log-likelihood units short of the optimum. Newton steps on the exact derivatives fixed this; the six fits examined then converged in 11 to 33 iterations.
- A bound bug was found and fixed during development: the branch length computed back from the bound value of s was 1.00000000000405 × 10⁻⁶, not exactly 10⁻⁶. A branch at its bound was therefore not recognised as active, and the optimiser stalled.
- **W2c dominates the cost:** 100 resamples × 3 topologies × 3 starts. A full 116-taxon run takes hours. This was not tuned away. Options for later, each needing the owner's decision because they change the pre-registered method or its cost:
  - start the resample fits from the full-data optimum instead of three starts, and measure the agreement;
  - subsample quartets (decision D4);
  - fewer resamples.
- W2-emp on Fish mtDNA uses π1 = 0.096 (the full MAW matrix is sparse). In all 15 quartet-topology fits of the cross-check (cross-check conditioning), its total fitted tree length is longer than under W2-sym.

## Known limitations

- Amalgamation, support, evaluation and figures are not yet stages of the analysis run.
- The hidden `--chunk-quartets` testing option is not stored in `run.json`; a resumed run calibrates chunk sizes again. Results do not depend on chunk sizes.
- Weight chunks need 60 bytes per quartet on disk (about 430 MB for 116 taxa).
