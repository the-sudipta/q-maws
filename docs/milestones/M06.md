# M6: Long-branch simulation (H3)

Status: **complete, awaiting the owner's approval**.

## What was built

- **Simulator** (`qmaws-core::sim`): the pre-registered four-taxon experiment of plan 2.7.6.
  - True tree ab|cd under the two-state symmetric model; leaf branches of a and c t_long ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 1.5}; other branches 0.05.
  - N ∈ {100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000} characters, simulated one by one along the tree (independently of the likelihood code). The 0000 columns are then removed.
  - 200 replicates per setting, each with a seed from SHA-256(global seed, setting, replicate).
- **Recovery** of ab|cd for W1, for W2 (W2-sym fitted) and, as a supplementary result, for W2c. Ties are shared: 1 ÷ k for k tied best topologies.
- **Figure** (`qmaws-viz::chart`): small-multiple SVG line charts, colour-blind safe, with line style and markers carrying the meaning as well.
- **Command** `qmaws simulate-h3`, writing `results/h3/recovery.csv`, `replicates.csv` (every seed and count), `recovery.svg` and `evaluation.txt` (the criterion applied mechanically).
- **Short report:** `results/h3/report.md`.

## Measured numbers (2026-10-02, owner's laptop, release build, one thread)

The full experiment (2,400 replicates, 100 W2c resamples each) took 60.8 s and 59.3 s in two runs. In the first run the longest setting took 7.9 s (t_long 1.5, N = 1,000).

## Findings

- **W1 is not just unreliable but consistently wrong** under these long branches. From N = 1,000 on it always chooses ac|bd, the topology that pairs the two long branches.
- **W2 is consistent here but needs many characters when branches are long.** For t_long = 1.5, recovery is 0.635 at N = 10,000 and 0.985 at N = 100,000.
- **At small N, W2 is also biased towards the long-branch topology:** 0.208 at t_long 1.5 and N = 100 is below the 1/3 of a random choice. The bias disappears as N grows.
- The risk listed in the plan, that conditioning only on "not 0000" might not identify the tree, did not appear in this design.

## Known limitations

- One tree shape, and data generated under the model that is fitted (W2-sym), as pre-registered. The experiment tests the weighting method, not how MAW characters behave.
- The figure is SVG only; PDF and PNG come with M10.
