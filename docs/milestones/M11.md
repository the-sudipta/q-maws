# M11: Full benchmark and comparison

Status: **in progress** (branch `m11`). This report is filled in as runs finish; every number in it comes from a logged run in `results/runs/` or from a cited published source in `baselines/published/`.

## What was built

- **Published baselines** (`baselines/published/`): ML-MAWS Table III, Table IV and values from its text; every tool's RF, nRF and nQD on the nine AFproject datasets (735 rows, with the raw answers of the results pages); Peafowl's nRF and run times. All labelled "reported", each row with its source.
- **H1 statistics** (`crates/qmaws-core/src/stats.rs`): exact one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (all `2^n` sign assignments enumerated, so tied ranks are handled exactly; zero differences dropped or Pratt), Holm correction, median, Hodges–Lehmann estimate, mean and sample standard deviation.
- **Summary command** (hidden, `qmaws summary`, `crates/qmaws-cli/src/summary_cmd.rs`): builds `results/summary/` from finished runs of the primary configuration and the published values; computes H1 with `--h1-pairs mean --h1-zeros wilcoxon` (Amendment 2) only when all 7 HGT datasets have 5 seeds; writes the AFproject upload files with `--afproject`.
- **Benchmark runs** (`results/runs/<dataset>_seed<n>/`): 14 datasets × 5 seeds, primary configuration (`M_full`, W2-sym, W2c with 100 resamples, wQFM-rs, S1). S2 (100 replicates, W2b inside) on seed 1 only. Fish mtDNA and E. coli/Shigella seed 1 are the M10 runs.

## Known limitations

- H2 and H4 are not part of M11: they are tested in M12 and M13 with ML-MAWS runs made by this project (D8, decided 2026-10-06). H1 in M11 uses the published ML-MAWS values only.
- S2 is computed on seed 1 only (except rhinovirus, which has S1 only); seeds 2–5 have S1 only.
- The NCBI datasets (influenza A, coronavirus, mammal mtDNA, ebolavirus, rhinovirus) have no reference tree in this project, so they have no nRF or nQD; their trees, support and run times are reported.
- With 7 datasets, the power of the H1 test is limited.
