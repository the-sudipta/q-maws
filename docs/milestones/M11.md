# M11: Full benchmark and comparison

Status: **complete and approved by the owner** (branch `m11-complete`). Every number in this report comes from a run in `results/runs/` (through `results/summary/`) or from a cited published source in `baselines/published/`.

## What was built

- **Published baselines** (`baselines/published/`): ML-MAWS Table III, Table IV and values from its text; every tool's RF, nRF and nQD on the nine AFproject datasets (735 rows, with the raw answers of the results pages); Peafowl's nRF and run times. All labelled "reported", each row with its source.
- **H1 statistics** (`crates/qmaws-core/src/stats.rs`): exact one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (all `2^n` sign assignments enumerated, so tied ranks are handled exactly; zero differences dropped or Pratt), Holm correction, median, Hodges–Lehmann estimate, mean and sample standard deviation.
- **Summary command** (hidden, `qmaws summary`, `crates/qmaws-cli/src/summary_cmd.rs`): builds `results/summary/` from the finished runs of the primary configuration and the published values; computes H1 with `--h1-pairs mean --h1-zeros wilcoxon` (Amendment 2); writes the AFproject upload files with `--afproject`.
- **Benchmark runs** (`results/runs/<dataset>_seed<n>/`): 14 datasets × 5 seeds, primary configuration (`M_full`, W2-sym, W2c with 100 resamples, wQFM-rs, S1). S2 (100 replicates, W2b inside) on seed 1 of every dataset except rhinovirus. Fish mtDNA and E. coli/Shigella seed 1 are the M10 runs (`fish_mito_2026-10-03_092551`, `ecoli_2026-10-03_080534`).
- **AFproject upload files** (`baselines/afproject_submission/`): the nine seed-1 trees, each byte-identical to its run's `report/tree.nwk`, with the upload pages and form fields. The upload is done by the owner; the scores AFproject returns are added there with the access date.

## Results

### H1

H1: under horizontal gene transfer, Q-MAWS has lower topological error than ML-MAWS (strand-aware, reported values). Seven HGT datasets; the Q-MAWS value of a pair is the mean over its 5 seeds; one-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test, zero differences dropped; Holm correction over the two metrics. Success criterion: Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 for at least one metric and median difference < 0.

| Metric | Pairs (zeros dropped) | W+ | W− | p (one-sided) | p (Holm) | Median difference | Hodges–Lehmann | Criterion met |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nRF | 7 (1) | 3 | 18 | 0.078125 | 0.156250 | −0.133333 | −0.103084 | no |
| nQD | 7 (0) | 28 | 0 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | +0.130946 | +0.135353 | no |

**H1 is not supported.** For nRF the median difference is negative (Q-MAWS lower) but the adjusted p-value is 0.156; for nQD Q-MAWS is higher on every dataset.

### Comparison per dataset

From `results/summary/comparison.tsv` (Q-MAWS mean ± sample SD over 5 seeds; published values as reported):

- **Lower nRF than ML-MAWS (strand-aware):** the four simulated datasets with HGT (250 to 1000 events): 0.513 to 0.567 against 0.700 to 0.733.
- **Equal nRF (within 0.001):** simulated HGT 0, E. coli/Shigella HGT, Yersinia HGT.
- **Higher nRF than ML-MAWS (losses):** Fish mtDNA 0.455 ± 0.000 against 0.409; E. coli/Shigella 0.638 ± 0.021 against 0.538.
- **nQD:** higher than ML-MAWS (strand-aware) on all nine datasets with a reference tree.
- **Against AFproject:** the best reported tool is better than Q-MAWS on every one of the nine datasets; the number of tools strictly better than Q-MAWS at AFproject's precision is in `comparison.tsv` (for example 102 of 122 on Fish mtDNA for nRF).
- **NCBI datasets** (influenza A, coronavirus, mammal mtDNA, ebolavirus, rhinovirus): no reference tree in this project, so no nRF or nQD; their trees, mean S1, seed-1 S2 and working times are in `qmaws.tsv`.

Seed variability (sample SD of nRF over the 5 seeds) is 0 for five of the nine reference datasets and at most 0.021; the SD of nQD is at most 0.066 (`qmaws.tsv`).

## How it was verified

- Every one of the 68 benchmark runs (`*_seed1` to `*_seed5`) passed `qmaws verify --quick` (input check, 20 recomputed chunks of each chunked stage and up to 3 bootstrap replicates, and the recomputed nRF, nQD and MSD); the report is in each run's `report/verify_*.txt`. The two M10 runs were verified in M10.
- Each run records its root fingerprint (`audit/root.txt`) and the build it was made with (`audit/environment.json`, `git_commit`).
- Continuous integration (formatting, lints, tests on Windows, macOS and Linux) ran on the pushed commits of `m11-complete`.

## How the runs were made

- One queue (`m11_queue.sh`, on the owner's laptop) ran the datasets in the order of the owner's decisions; from 2026-10-06 a second worker ran rhinovirus at the same time until the main queue reached the NCBI datasets. Runs were stopped by laptop shutdowns many times and resumed from their saved chunks; a resumed run gives the same root fingerprint as an uninterrupted one (golden test G11).
- Three builds were used, all with the same computation: commit 7b1de8a until 2026-10-07 11:51 UTC; 5f0cd87 (7b1de8a with every thread given work in small chunks) until 2026-10-11 15:23 UTC; then 5943bcc (5f0cd87 asking Windows not to throttle the program in the background). Each change was made with the owner's approval after the builds gave the same root fingerprints on test runs; each run records its build.

## Measured numbers (owner's laptop: Intel Core 5 210H, 12 logical cores, 8 GB RAM; release builds)

Mean working time per dataset over the 5 seeds (`qmaws.tsv`, `elapsed_mean_seconds`): from 89 s (Yersinia HGT, 8 taxa) to 52,483 s (rhinovirus, 116 taxa). **These times are not comparable between runs** and are not used for any timing claim: runs were made with three builds, with and without a second worker on the same laptop, on battery and on mains power, and most of them while Windows ran the hidden queue in efficiency mode (about ten times slower; see "Build changes in detail" below). Timing is the subject of M13.

## Known limitations

- H1 uses the ML-MAWS values reported in its paper, as pre-registered; same-device ML-MAWS runs belong to H2 and H4 (D8).
- S2 is computed on seed 1 only (none for rhinovirus); seeds 2–5 have S1 only.
- The NCBI datasets have no reference tree in this project, so they give no topological error.
- With 7 datasets, the power of the H1 test is limited.
- The AFproject scores of the upload files are not yet available (upload by the owner).

## Build changes in detail

Kept from the working notes of M11 (`docs/M11_RESUME_NOTES.md`, removed when M11 was complete).

### Binary change (2026-10-07)
The weighing stage splits each chunk of quartets into blocks of 16 that run in parallel. The calibrated chunks of M11 are small (21 to 25 quartets for the simulated HGT datasets, 82 for rhinovirus), so only 2 to 6 of the 12 threads had work and the laptop ran at about 35% CPU. Commit 5f0cd87 (on 7b1de8a, branch `m11-perf`) changes only `run_blocks` in `crates/qmaws-engine/src/analysis.rs`: a chunk is cut into at least one block per thread. Records are still joined in position order, so their bytes do not depend on the blocks.

Checks before the switch, all on this laptop:
- Root fingerprints of the 7b1de8a build and the 5f0cd87 build are identical: Yersinia HGT seed 3 without S2 (`8502148d...`), Fish mtDNA seed 3 without S2 (`9f7445aa...`), and Fish mtDNA with 21-quartet chunks (`9f7445aa...`).
- A Fish mtDNA run killed under the 7b1de8a build (452 weighing chunks done) and resumed with the 5f0cd87 build gave the same root (`9f7445aa...`).
- The engine tests of 5f0cd87 pass (77).
- Speed: Fish mtDNA with 21-quartet chunks took 698 s with the 7b1de8a build and 125 s with the 5f0cd87 build (both while the M11 queues were running).

The switch: at 11:51 UTC both queues and their runs were stopped, `qmaws_m11.exe` was renamed `qmaws_m11_7b1de8a.exe` (kept), the 5f0cd87 build was copied to `qmaws_m11.exe`, and both queues were started again; `sim_hgt_1000_seed3` and `rhinovirus_seed5` resumed from their saved chunks. Each run records the build in `audit/environment.json` (`git_commit`): runs finished before the switch show 7b1de8a; `sim_hgt_1000_seed3`, `rhinovirus_seed5` and every later run show 5f0cd87.

### Windows power throttling (2026-10-08)
Windows 11 runs a program without a visible window (as the queues are started, hidden) in "efficiency mode": on the slow cores at a low clock speed. Measured on this laptop with the same build (5f0cd87 with timing output) and the same Fish mtDNA weighing in chunks of 82 quartets: 0.42 s per chunk from a console, 4.92 s launched hidden, 0.35 s launched hidden with a fix that asks Windows not to throttle the program (`SetProcessInformation` with `ProcessPowerThrottling`, in `crates/qmaws-cli/src/main.rs`; no change to the computation). On the rhinovirus data the hidden run needed 3.22 s per chunk against 0.33 s from a console. The M11 runs so far ran throttled; their results do not depend on it.

Builds with the fix, each its earlier commit plus only this change, and each giving the same root fingerprints as the build it replaces (Yersinia HGT seed 3 without S2, `8502148d...`; Fish mtDNA seed 3 without S2, `9f7445aa...` for M11):
- M11: commit 5943bcc (branch `m11-perf`, on 5f0cd87). With the owner's approval, the M11 queue switched to it on 2026-10-11 at 15:23 UTC: the queue and its run were stopped, `qmaws_m11.exe` was renamed `qmaws_m11_5f0cd87.exe` (kept), the 5943bcc build was copied to `qmaws_m11.exe`, and the queue was started again; `ebolavirus_seed2` resumed from its saved chunks. Runs finished after the switch record `git_commit` 5943bcc in `audit/environment.json`.
- M12 and M13: first commit b8782e2 (branch `m12-run`, on 07b3a8a).
