# H3: long-branch simulation

**Hypothesis (pre-registered, `docs/PREREGISTRATION.md`):** informative-pattern voting (W1) is misled under long-branch conditions; the conditioned quartet likelihood (W2) is not.

**Criterion:** in every setting where W1 recovery is below 50% at N = 100,000 characters, W2 recovery is at least 95% at N = 100,000.

**Result: supported.** At N = 100,000 characters:
- W1 recovered the true quartet in 0 of 200 replicates for each of the three long-branch lengths, so the criterion applies in all three settings.
- W2 recovered it in 100%, 100% and 98.5% of replicates, so the criterion is met in all three.

## Design

As pre-registered (plan 2.7.6):

- **Tree and model:** true tree ab|cd under the two-state symmetric model. The leaf branches of a and c have length t_long ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 1.5}; the leaf branches of b and d and the internal branch have length 0.05. a and c are not sisters, so they form the long-branch attraction pair.
- **Characters:** N ∈ {100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000} characters are simulated per replicate, then the 0000 columns are removed (mimicking MAW ascertainment). The number of columns left is in `recovery.csv`.
- **Simulation:** each character is simulated along the tree, with change probability (1 − exp(−2t)) ÷ 2 per branch. The simulator is independent of the likelihood code it tests; a test checks that its pattern frequencies match the model's pattern probabilities.
- **Replicates and seeds:** 200 replicates per setting. Seed of each replicate: the first 8 bytes of SHA-256(global seed 1, index of t_long, index of N, replicate number), recorded per replicate in `replicates.csv`.
- **Methods:**
  - W1: the topology with the highest count of its two split patterns.
  - W2: the W2-sym conditioned likelihood ("not 0000"), with the optimiser of M5.
  - W2c, supplementary: the topology with the highest W2c weight, from 100 resamples seeded with `quartet_seed(replicate seed, 1)`; a resample whose three fits have the internal branch at the lower bound counts as a three-way tie (rule added in M8, `docs/OPEN_ISSUES.md` OI-14).
- **Recovery** of a replicate: 1 if ab|cd is the only best topology, 1 ÷ k if it is one of k tied best topologies (the expected value of a random choice among them), 0 otherwise. W1 with no split pattern counts as a tie of all three. W2 ties are log-likelihoods within 10⁻⁸. The number of replicates with ties is reported.

## Interpretation

- **W1 fails completely, as expected under long-branch attraction.** From N = 1,000 on, W1 never recovers the true quartet. Instead it chooses ac|bd, which pairs the two long branches: the W1 score of ac|bd is strictly the highest in all 1,800 replicates with N ≥ 1,000, and in 571 of the 600 with N = 100 (`replicates.csv`). More data makes W1 more certain of the wrong answer.
- **W2 converges to the true topology.** Its recovery rises with N in every setting and reaches 0.985 to 1.000 at N = 100,000.
  - In this design, conditioning on "not 0000" does not prevent consistency. The risk listed in the plan, that this conditioning might be insufficient for identifiability, was not observed here.
- **Convergence is slow for the longest branches.** At t_long = 1.5, W2 needs about 100,000 characters to reach 95%: it recovers 0.635 at N = 10,000.
- **At small N, W2 is also drawn towards the wrong topology:** 0.208 at t_long = 1.5 and N = 100 is below the 1/3 of a random choice. The finite-sample bias is in the same direction as W1's, but much weaker, and it disappears as N grows.
- **W2c behaves like W2 at large N,** and is lower than W2 at small N (for example 0.140 against 0.208 at t_long = 1.5, N = 100).

## Limitations

- Only one tree shape, one model (W2-sym, the generating model) and three long-branch lengths, as pre-registered. Real MAW data are not generated by this model; H3 tests the weighting method, not the MAW characters.
- The figure is SVG only; PDF and PNG exports come with the figure milestone (M10).

## Reproduce

`qmaws simulate-h3 --output results/h3` (global seed 1, 200 replicates, 100 W2c resamples; 60.8 s and 59.3 s in two runs on the owner's laptop, one thread). Two runs gave byte-identical `recovery.csv`, `replicates.csv` and `recovery.svg`. A test reproduces one committed replicate exactly on every CI platform. Re-run on 2026-10-03 after the W2c tie rule of OI-14 (M8): W1 and W2 are unchanged, only the W2c column changed.
